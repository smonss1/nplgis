Team plan

Five phases, one shared plan

Each phase ends with a concrete checkpoint so the next step is clear.

P0Now → 3 Oct

Enrol, form your team, choose a topic

Checkpoint: enrolled and topic chosen

  • Enrol online, download the terms and conditions, and choose a team number. Teams have 2–6 members; one team per student.
  • Attend the 3 Oct AI Workshop. The theme is “AI-empowered Spatial Data”; AI should be integral, not decorative.
  • Choose a topic around smart city, sustainability, community, future applications, human care, or spaces we love.
  • Assign team roles before work begins.
P14 → 14 Oct · pre-test

Data and analysis sprint

Checkpoint: analysis done by 14 Oct

  • Category 3 workshop is 10 Oct. Category 2 teams can prepare data first and attend the 31 Oct workshop.
  • Fetch CSDI portal data and run the analysis. Category 3: buffer, clip, overlay, spatial join, and a thematic map. Category 2: examine distribution patterns and read the map; analysis is optional.
  • Stop by 14 Oct. Pause project work during the 15–22 Oct term test.
P223 Oct → 15 Nov

Build the StoryMap

Checkpoint: full draft v1 by 15 Nov

  • Build a map-centred narrative with geotagged images or video and clear text sections.
  • Category 2 teams attend the 31 Oct workshop.
  • Draft the slide deck (10 pages maximum) in parallel rather than leaving it until December.
  • Review against the rubric: Content 25, Research 25, Creativity 20, AI 15, Presentation 15.
P316 → 30 Nov

Video, AI declaration, and quality checks

Checkpoint: submission-ready by 30 Nov

  • Record and edit the one-minute video; check that the sharing link works.
  • Write the AI-use statement: name each tool, explain what it did, and say how outputs were verified.
  • Write the data-validation statement and distinguish AI-inferred ai_ fields from CSDI source fields.
  • Self-check against the rubric and improve map readability.
P41 → 5 Dec

Final checks and submission

Submit by 5 Dec, 23:59

  • 1–3 Dec: open the web map in a private window, click every link, and test the video.
  • 4 Dec: final read-through and a school-side, five-minute mock pitch.
  • 5 Dec: email the web map HTML source or long URL, slide deck, and video link with your team number to csdiawards2026@csdigeolab.gov.hk.
  • Enrolment also closes on this date. No edits are allowed after submitting.
